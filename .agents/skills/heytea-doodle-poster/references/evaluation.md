# Evaluation Guide

Use this guide for human review after generating a poster.

## Photo-entry pass criteria

When a usable photo arrives without an explicit output request, offer exactly:

1. 生成带字版海报
2. 生成不带字海报

Do not generate before the user chooses. An explicit text-poster, no-text-poster,
or both-posters request bypasses this choice.

## Pass criteria

- One real object remains recognizable and photographic.
- The background is white or off-white with strong negative space.
- The chosen template is clear before generation.
- Black primitive micro-worker lines interact with the object instead of floating randomly.
- The doodles feel naive, functional, and hand-drawn rather than polished or mascot-like.
- No official HEYTEA logos, marks, watermarks, or affiliation claims appear.
- The final image feels quiet, healing, playful, and publishable.

For `带字版`:

- The title is a primary composition element, not a small caption.
- Chinese characters have rough, crooked, childlike glyph skeletons rather than a clean font skeleton.
- The object and micro-worker support the title instead of competing with it.
- Use a separate title-layer workflow when lettering accuracy matters.

For `无字版`:

- There is no text, lettering, number, caption, label, signature, or watermark.
- The composition does not reserve a title block.
- The object and micro-worker form a complete small action scene.

## Common failure modes

- Full cartoon conversion that loses the object's photographic texture.
- Decorative clutter, stickers, bubbles, or unrelated props.
- Clean vector icons, gradients, UI-card composition, or generic poster polish.
- Typography version with clean standard-font lettering.
- No-text version that is only the typography version with its title removed.
- Micro-worker drifting into a cute mascot or chibi character.
- Invented brand marks or unreadable text noise.

## Review questions

1. Is the real object still the hero?
2. Is the selected template visually obvious?
3. Does the micro-worker perform a readable action?
4. For `带字版`, does the title have a handmade glyph structure?
5. For `无字版`, does the story work without any text?
6. Is there enough white space?
